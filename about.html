About Pinnacle Shield Insurance

Four decades of protecting what matters most to American families.

Our Story

Founded in 1985 by Eleanor and Thomas Whitfield, Pinnacle Shield Insurance began as a small family agency nestled in the heart of Hartford, Connecticut — the historic insurance capital of the United States. With little more than a shared belief that every family deserves honest, affordable protection, the Whitfields built their business on a foundation of trust, transparency, and genuine care for their clients.

Over the past four decades, Pinnacle Shield has grown from a two-person storefront into a nationally recognized carrier serving customers in all 50 states. Our Hartford headquarters now employs over 1,200 dedicated professionals, while our network of licensed agents spans every major metropolitan area in the country. Despite our growth, we have never lost sight of the personal touch that defined us from day one.

Today, Pinnacle Shield offers comprehensive Auto, Home, and Life insurance products backed by industry-leading underwriters and award-winning claims support. We are proud holders of an A+ rating from AM Best and have been recognized by Insurance Business America as a Top 10 Carrier for Customer Satisfaction five years running. Whether you are protecting your first home, insuring a new vehicle, or planning your family's financial future, Pinnacle Shield is here for every milestone, every mile, and every moment.
